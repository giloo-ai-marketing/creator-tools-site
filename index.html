How it works

Four steps. You keep every decision that matters.

四個步驟。重要的決定都還在你手上。

01Upload the film上傳影片The cut, the trailer, subtitles, stills. It goes into your own space and nobody else sees it.正片、預告、字幕、劇照。放進你自己的空間，別人看不到。
02Say what you need說出你要什麼One sentence, in your words. Or pick the one closest to your situation.一句話，用你自己的講法。或挑一個最接近的情況。
03Check before it runs開始前先確認It shows what it will do and what it costs. Nothing starts until you press start.它會先說要做什麼、花多少。你按開始才會動。
04Approve, or ask for changes核准，或叫它改Changes are free. When it is right, it is saved to the film and ready to send.修改不另外收費。改好之後存回這部片，可以直接寄出去。

Frequently asked

The questions people ask in the first five minutes.

大家前五分鐘都會問的問題。

What does it cost?要收費嗎？
Free while it is a prototype, for creators with one film. In exchange we ask for honest reactions. Later, the people paying are distributors and rights holders with a catalogue, not directors with one film.
原型階段對一部片的創作者免費，交換條件是誠實的反應。之後付錢的會是手上有一批片的發行商和版權方，不是只有一部片的導演。
Will my film be used to train AI?我的片會被拿去訓練 AI 嗎？
No. Your film is used to do the job you asked for, in your own space, and nothing else. If we ever want to do anything beyond that, we will ask you in writing first.
不會。你的片只用來做你要求的那件事，跑在你自己的空間裡。如果哪天我們想做別的用途，會先用書面問你。
My English is not good. Can I still use it?我英文不好，可以用嗎？
Yes. The interface and everything it produces come in Chinese and English, and subtitles also cover Japanese and Korean. You write in whichever language you think in.
可以。介面和產出都有中英文，字幕另外支援日文和韓文。你用你習慣思考的語言寫就好。
My film is not finished. Too early?我的片還沒完成，太早了嗎？
Not at all. The earliest useful moment is the day you lock picture, when you start thinking about where it goes. Some of it works during post as well.
不會。最早能用的時間點是剪定版那天，開始想片子要去哪裡的時候。有些功能在後製階段就能用。
Does this replace my sales agent or distributor?這會取代我的銷售代理或發行商嗎？
No, and it is not trying to. Relationships with programmers and buyers are built by people. This handles the material, the admin and the research around those relationships so your time goes into the conversations.
不會，也沒有要這樣做。跟策展人和買家的關係是靠人建立的。這個工具處理的是那些關係周邊的素材、行政和查資料，讓你的時間花在對話上。
Who is behind it?是誰在做？
Giloo, a streaming platform for independent and documentary film based in Taipei, working with festivals, distributors and creators since 2019.
Giloo，一個做獨立與紀錄影像的串流平台，在台北，從 2019 年開始跟影展、片商和創作者合作。
